What do the three parameters measure?
  • Knowledge: recalling facts, terms and basic concepts from the lesson.
  • Understanding: explaining ideas in their own words and grasping the meaning behind them.
  • Application: using what was learned to solve new problems or real-life situations.

Scoring each one separately shows where a student is strong or weak, so teaching and support can focus on the right area.
